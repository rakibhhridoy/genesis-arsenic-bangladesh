================================================================================
METHODS STACK
================================================================================

1. DATA CURATION & HARMONIZATION
   - Standardize units, parameter names across GEMStat/Podgorski/primary
   - Quality filtering (CBE, detection limits, outlier removal)
   - Geochemical vector representation: each sample = vector of
     [As, Fe, Mn, PO4, pH, Eh, EC, TDS, Ca, Mg, Na, K, Cl, HCO3, SO4, NO3]
   - Missing value handling via masked tokens (BERT-style)

2. SELF-SUPERVISED PRETRAINING
   Option A: Masked Geochemical Modeling (MGM)
   - Randomly mask 15-30% of parameters in each sample
   - Train encoder-decoder to reconstruct masked values
   - Learns latent geochemical relationships globally
   - Similar to BERT but for geochemical vectors

   Option B: Contrastive Geochemical Learning
   - Positive pairs: samples from same well/station at different times
   - Negative pairs: samples from different hydrogeological settings
   - Learns representation that captures geochemical similarity

   Option C: Both (two-stage pretraining)

3. LATENT DIFFUSION MODEL FOR FUTURE-STATE GENERATION
   - Encode geochemical state into latent space (pretrained encoder)
   - Condition on: current geochemistry, depth, geology, land use,
     time horizon, climate variables
   - Diffusion process generates distribution of future geochemical states
   - Output: probabilistic prediction with full uncertainty
   - Inspired by Zhan et al. (2025 GRL) but for geochemistry, not flow

4. FEW-SHOT FINE-TUNING
   - Freeze pretrained encoder
   - Fine-tune prediction head on Bangladesh temporal pairs (224 chemistry-complete wells)
   - Evaluate: how many local samples needed for useful prediction?
   - Target: <50 samples for meaningful temporal prediction

5. ZERO-SHOT TRANSFER
   - Apply pretrained model to Red River Delta, Mekong, etc.
   - No local training data required
   - Evaluate: does the model capture known contamination patterns?

6. PHYSICAL CONSISTENCY CHECKS
   - Charge balance of generated geochemical states
   - Thermodynamic feasibility (SI checks via PHREEQC)
   - Redox consistency (Eh-Fe-Mn relationships)

================================================================================
KEY RISKS & MITIGATIONS
================================================================================

Risk: GEMStat data quality is heterogeneous
Mitigation: Aggressive quality filtering; pretraining is robust to
noise (BERT-style models handle noisy data well); ablation on
data quality thresholds

Risk: Geochemical data is NOT images/text — foundation model
paradigm may not transfer
Mitigation: Geochemical vectors have strong internal structure
(charge balance, redox coupling) that self-supervised learning
can exploit; start with masked autoencoder (simplest approach)

Risk: Zero-shot transfer may fail across very different geologies
Mitigation: Report honestly; show where it works and where it
doesn't; even partial transferability is novel

Risk: Reviewers question "foundation model" framing for tabular data
Mitigation: Emphasize: (a) scale of pretraining data (26M+),
(b) emergent cross-geography generalization, (c) few-shot capability
— these are the hallmarks of foundation models regardless of modality

Risk: Compute requirements for pretraining
Mitigation: Geochemical vectors are small (16-20 features) compared
to images/text; pretraining on 26M tabular rows is feasible on
a single GPU in days
