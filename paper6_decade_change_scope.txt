WORKING TITLE (draft)
"Improving means, worsening tails: decade-scale divergence in global
 groundwater arsenic and nitrate"

CORE FINDING
Mean contaminant trends and risk-tail (WHO-threshold exceedance) trends
DECOUPLE, and the direction of decoupling is country-specific:
  - USA: median dAs ~0 (flat) BUT dangerous tail GREW +75 wells (707->782
    above 10 ug/L). Improving-looking mean hides growing risk. (n=3927)
  - Mexico: worsens on BOTH axes (+4.3 median, +81 tail, p=4e-189). (n=3311)
  - France: genuine bulk improvement (median -1.75) tail roughly stable. (n=9032)
  - Spain/Italy: tail shrank while median held (the encouraging pattern).
NO3 continental story:
  - Declining across regulated Europe (Germany -2.3, Poland -0.5, Czechia,
    Serbia, Lithuania; all p<<0.001) — nitrate-directive era.
  - Rising in France (+0.20, n=28604, p=6e-112), USA, Greece, India —
    intensifying / less-regulated agriculture.

DATA
- 58,722 temporal pairs, median 14-yr gap, from genesis.duckdb
  (genesis_temporal_pairs). Wilcoxon signed-rank per country.
- Global As-change map (2x2 deg cells): figures/fig_decade_as_map.png
- Mean-vs-tail paradox scatter: figures/fig_decade_tail_paradox.png
- Country stats: results/decade_change.json

KNOWN CAVEATS / TODO BEFORE WRITING
- Canada dAs +140 (n=108) is a uranium/metal-MINING-region artifact -> exclude
  or annotate; not a trend.
- Coverage is Global-North heavy (FR, USA, Mexico, EU); NO Bangladesh in this
  global temporal set (BD is the separate held-out). Frame scope honestly.
- Observational, NOT causal: driver analysis weak (aridity strongest dAs
  correlate rho -0.17; population/GRACE-twsa/precip <0.08). Either find a
  cleaner attribution angle or frame as a descriptive change-atlas.
- As measurement heterogeneity (field-kit vs ICP-MS) affects some sources;
  control or restrict to consistent-method subsets.
- Consider depth stratification (only Bangladesh has depth; global lacks it).

WHY PUBLISHABLE
- Large multi-decade, multi-country paired-well dataset is rare.
- The mean-vs-tail decoupling is a non-obvious, policy-relevant message
  (monitoring a mean can mask growing exceedance risk).
- Complements the regulated-vs-intensifying NO3 narrative.
